Meet The Artist

A Little Art, A Lot of Love.

Welcome to Samira's Henna Artwork, a creative space dedicated to the beauty of henna. From delicate floral patterns to detailed traditional designs, every artwork is an opportunity to turn a simple moment into a memorable one. Alongside my artwork, I also offer organic henna products for henna lovers.

My Artistic Style

I blend timeless subcontinental motifs—like jaali latticework, blooming paisleys, and lotus mandalas—with contemporary, airy negative spaces and delicate finger jewelry patterns. Each design is custom-drawn freehand to complement the unique shape of your hands.

Naturally Inspired Henna Products

To ensure that my clients and fellow artists enjoy safe, aromatic, and rich dark stains, I hand-mix small batches of organic henna cones using triple-sifted natural leaf powder, pure essential oils, and fresh botanical extracts. No chemical dyes, no black henna, and zero PPD.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering products and booking henna appointments.

Browse our Organic Henna section, select your desired quantity on the product card, and click the "Order Now on WhatsApp" button. This will automatically open WhatsApp with a formatted message containing the product name, quantity, and subtotal. Once you send the message, Samira will confirm stock availability, delivery charge, and payment options directly with you.

If you are planning to apply henna immediately for an upcoming event, our Fresh Organic Henna Cones are ready to use. If you like mixing your own custom paste or want powder with long shelf life, choose the Pure Organic Henna Powder. For brides, gifting, or beginners wanting the complete experience, the Henna Lover's Artistry Combo includes cones, powder, aftercare oil, and stencils at the best value.

You can reach out directly via the "Chat on WhatsApp" button in our Contact section or click on any gallery design in our portfolio to inquire about that specific style. Please share your event date, preferred location, and the number of people to check availability and obtain a personalized quote.

In the automatically generated WhatsApp message, simply fill in your full name, complete delivery address (with district and area), and your contact phone number. If you need delivery before a specific celebration date, please mention that in the additional notes.

Delivery charges depend on your delivery location (e.g. inside Dhaka or outside Dhaka) and the chosen courier service. When you send your WhatsApp order with your delivery area, Samira will calculate and confirm the exact delivery charge before taking your confirmation.
